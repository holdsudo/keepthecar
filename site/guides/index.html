    Ally is the bank behind leases on a lot of badges, which is why the question comes up so often. The answer is the same for every one of them: the residual is a contract number. The negotiation is everywhere else.

    Every Tesla lease buyout thread is a pile of answers that were true for somebody else's agreement in some other year. Yours has one source of truth, and it is not a forum.

    A retailer's offer on your leased car is only worth something if the leasing company will take the retailer's money. Many will not, and the offer is a reseller's number either way.

  • MechanicsThird-Party Lease Buyout: Dealer vs Finance Company vs You

  • FeesLease Buyout Fees: Which Are Real and Which Are Padding

    A lease buyout has two legitimate costs on top of the residual: the fee your contract names and the tax and title charges your state collects. Everything else on the sheet is somebody's margin.

  • The mathHow to Calculate a Lease Buyout (Your Real Number, Not the Dealer's)

    Your buyout is five numbers added together, and four of them are printed in your lease or on your state's fee schedule. The dealer's version adds a sixth, and the sixth is theirs.

  • DecisionShould I Buy Out My Lease, Trade It In, or Return It?

    Three doors at the end of a lease: buy it, trade it, return it. One of them keeps your equity, one hands it to the dealer, and one pays the leasing company to take the car back.

  • TimingEarly Lease Buyout: How Early, What It Costs, When It's Dumb

    An early buyout is the rest of your lease paid in one check. It is rarely a bargain, sometimes the right move, and always a number you should get from the leasing company, not the dealer.

  • Taxes: New YorkNew York Sales Tax on a Lease Buyout: Yes, You Pay It Again

    The sales tax you paid during the lease was tax on the payments. The buyout is a new sale, and New York taxes it again, on the full buyout price, at the rate where the car is registered.

  • Taxes: ConnecticutConnecticut Sales Tax on a Lease Buyout

    Connecticut treats a lease buyout as what it is, a sale of a used car to you, and taxes it on the buyout price. The tax you paid during the lease does not cover it.

  • Taxes: MarylandMaryland Lease Buyout Tax: The 6% Excise Tax, Explained

    Maryland does not call it sales tax, and that confuses people into thinking there is none. There is a 6% vehicle excise tax, collected by the MVA when the car is titled, and a lease buyout is a title transfer.

  • Taxes: VirginiaVirginia Lease Buyout Tax: SUT on Your Payoff

    Virginia's Motor Vehicle Sales and Use Tax, SUT on the DMV forms, is charged when a vehicle is titled to a new owner. A lease buyout titles the car to you, so SUT is charged on the buyout price.

  • Taxes: CaliforniaCalifornia Sales Tax on a Lease Buyout: The DMV Does the Collecting

    California taxes the buyout of a leased car as a purchase, at the combined rate for the address where the car is registered, and the DMV collects it when the title moves to you. The tax you paid during the lease covered the payments, not the car.

    A buyout package is the leasing company's side of the sale: a price with a deadline and the forms the state wants. Here is what every page does and which pages are not really part of it.

  • TitleLease Buyout Title Transfer: How the Title Actually Gets to You

    The leasing company owns the car until the day the payoff clears. Getting the title from its name into yours is a separate process with its own clock, and nobody at a bank or credit union runs it for you.

  • ComparisonLease Then Buy Out vs Finance From Day One

    Both paths end with you owning the same car. One pays tax, title, fees, and interest once. The other pays most of them twice. Here is how to add it up and when the second path still wins.
